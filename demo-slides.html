02The problem: a museum nobody visits

  • Every Moon and Mars mission left machinery behind — landers, rovers, instruments, even golf balls.
  • Most people never learn their names or what they accomplished.
  • Their stories are scattered across decades of mission reports and archives.
  • As spaceflight grows crowded, questions of heritage, debris, and responsibility are arriving fast.

Visual: split frame — Apollo descent stage photo (NASA, credited) beside a Mars rover; question mark between "mission" and "memory."

03The solution: a museum that fits in a browser tab

Explore

Interactive Moon/Mars map with clickable landing-site pins (positions labeled approximate).

Listen

16 first-person artifact stories with real mission facts — press play and the browser reads them aloud.

Remember

Leave a one-line epitaph on a memorial wall — held in memory only, nothing stored or shared.

Test

A quiz generated live from the dataset, plus a Then-vs-Now timeline from 1967 to 2024.

Visual: short screen recording — scroll hero → toggle Moon/Mars → click pin → story card → Listen → memorial tribute → quiz.

04How NASA data makes it possible

  • NASA Image and Video Library API — live imagery + credit for every artifact at runtime, with graceful fallback.
  • NASA NSSDC Master Catalog — verified mission dates, spacecraft records, locations.
  • LRO / LROC + mission pages — landing-site context and mission histories.
  • Every artifact carries a source note stating exactly what to verify — facts stay honest.

Visual: diagram — artifacts.json → app.js → NASA API → credited image on card; side arrow to NSSDC.

05Key features

  • Zero build: plain HTML, CSS, vanilla JS — no frameworks, no backend, no API keys.
  • Offline-capable: embedded fallback data and original SVG illustrations.
  • Accessible: semantic HTML, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support.
  • Privacy-first: no accounts, no tracking, no cookies — the memorial wall is memory-only.
  • Lightweight: small footprint, lazy-loaded images, Lighthouse-friendly.

Visual: checklist graphic; optional Lighthouse score screenshot.

06Impact

  • Turns datasheets into emotional, memorable stories for students and the public.
  • Builds awareness of off-world heritage sites and preservation debates.
  • Models responsible exploration: what we leave behind matters.
  • Runs anywhere — classrooms, museums, hackathons — with zero infrastructure.
