    Why it matters

    Space heritage

    The Apollo landing sites and early Mars landers are humanity's first off-world cultural heritage. They mark where we crossed a threshold.

    Preservation

    These sites may deserve protection. Some nations have proposed treating Apollo sites as preserved heritage zones, like museums under open sky.

    Space debris

    Each abandoned machine raises a real question: what do we leave behind, and who decides? Mars and the Moon have no cleanup crews — yet.

    Planetary protection

    Abandoned equipment carries Earth microbes. Understanding contamination helps us protect possible Martian environments — and search for life safely.

    Responsible exploration

    The artifacts on this page are reminders that exploration leaves footprints. Future missions — crewed and robotic — should leave less, not more.
